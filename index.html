Heylo (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the Heylo mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use an account on Heylo, we may collect:

  • Name – to create and display your profile.
  • Email address – to create and manage your account and for account-related communication.
  • Profile information – information you choose to add to your Heylo profile.
  • Profile photo – if you choose to upload one.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve the App’s functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Heylo account.
  • Display your profile to other users according to the App’s functionality.
  • Provide and maintain Heylo’s services.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account.
  • Improve the performance, security, and functionality of the App.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Your profile information and profile photo may be visible to other Heylo users depending on the features and privacy settings of the App.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Heylo, such as hosting, database, authentication, or storage providers. These providers are only used to support the operation of the App.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide Heylo’s services and maintain your account, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Heylo account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us at heyloofficial506@gmail.com. After verifying the request, we will delete your account and associated personal information, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.

7. Children’s Privacy

Heylo is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
